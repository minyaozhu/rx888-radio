What's on 1.0–1.7 GHz

A sweep of the top of the RX-888's VHF/UHF tuner (it reaches 1700 MHz) from this antenna: each 7.4 MHz tuner window watched for a moment, levels in dB above that window's noise floor, the strongest in each 100 kHz. Below the chart: what each band could give, what it would take, and what this antenna can't reach.

“Survey live” sweeps the range on the chart: it needs the receiver, so it pauses the voice session (pages listening on VHF / UHF, P25 included, lose their audio meanwhile) and puts the tuner back afterwards. The whole 1000–1700 MHz takes about 3 minutes at 1.5 s per window; a zoomed range only its part. The tuner is less sensitive up here, and most of these signals come from satellites or aircraft, so a weak or empty sweep is expected without a dedicated antenna.
