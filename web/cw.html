    Each band is one 48 kHz-wide USB channel covering 160 m 1.817–1.841, 80 m 3.515–3.539, 40 m 7.010–7.034, 30 m 10.103–10.127, 20 m 14.010–14.034, 17 m 18.070–18.094, 15 m 21.010–21.034, 12 m 24.890–24.914, 10 m 28.010–28.034 and 6 m 50.080–50.104 MHz (6 m with the 0–64 MHz coverage). Signals closer than about 100 Hz blur into one. The decoder learns each sender's speed from its dots and dashes, so the first few characters can be wrong; machine-sent CW decodes best. The skimmer runs only while the receiver is on its HF input. SNR is the key-down level over the noise in a 47 Hz bin.
