SSTV Pictures

Pictures sent by radio amateurs on the SSTV calling frequencies, decoded on this Mac. Each frequency is watched for SSTV's tones; a transmission is captured from 40 s before it's spotted until it ends, and decoded (Martin, Scottie, Robot, PD and other modes). Busiest on weekend daytimes, on 14.230 MHz.

The detector looks for SSTV's 1200 Hz line-sync pulses (the bar; a capture starts above the mark at 0.25) and most energy in the 1100–2350 Hz picture band. A capture without a decodable picture is dropped. A picture already under way when the monitor starts is missed: the decoder needs the opening tone burst (VIS header) that names the mode.
