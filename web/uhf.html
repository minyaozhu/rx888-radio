What's on 200–1000 MHz

A sweep of the RX-888's VHF/UHF tuner from this antenna: a 3-second look at every 4 MHz window, then 20 seconds on the bands that looked promising. Levels are dB above each window's noise floor. Below the chart: what each band would give as a tab, what it would take, and what isn't worth it (or isn't allowed).

The chart starts from the survey of one morning (the cards and the signals table below are from it). “Survey live” sweeps the range on the chart again now and redraws it window by window: it needs the receiver, so it pauses the voice session (pages listening on VHF lose their audio meanwhile). The whole 200–1000 MHz takes about 3 minutes at 1.5 s per window; a zoomed range only its part.
