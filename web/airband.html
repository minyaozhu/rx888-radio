      Transcript

        Transcripts are raw Whisper (large-v3-turbo) output from noisy AM audio, shown twice: plain, and with an air-traffic vocabulary hint, which helps with call signs and phrases but can also pull a garbled call towards stock phrases. Flights and phases are picked out automatically from that text (airline name + number, or a 3–4 digit number ending a read-back; key words like "descend", "heading", "in sight", "cleared", "contact tower"), so expect misses and mix-ups. Flights heard on an airport's tower or ground frequency sit at that airport; flights heard on NorCal Approach sit along the SFO Quiet Bridge arrival flow at the furthest stage they've been told, which only fits SFO arrivals. The receiver alternates between the approach window (131.6–137 MHz) and the tower window (118–124.8 MHz), so each is heard about half the time. Frequencies from AirNav (FAA). Received on an RX-888 MkII through radiod; every channel in the tuner's 8.4 MHz window at once.
