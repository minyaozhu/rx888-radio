      Transcript

        Transcripts are raw Whisper (large-v3-turbo) output from noisy AM audio, shown twice: plain, and with an air-traffic vocabulary hint, which helps with call signs and phrases but can also pull a garbled call towards stock phrases. Flights and phases are picked out automatically from that text (airline name + number, or a 3–4 digit number ending a read-back; key words like "descend", "heading", "in sight", "cleared", "contact tower"), so expect misses and mix-ups. The map is the San Francisco 28L/28R arrival flow; a flight sits at the furthest stage it has been told, which only fits arrivals. Received on an RX-888 MkII through radiod; every channel in the tuner's 8.4 MHz window at once.
