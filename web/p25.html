The region's 700 MHz digital trunked radio system. One control channel assigns each call to a voice channel; dsd-fme decodes the control channel, follows unencrypted calls to their voice channel and records them. Encrypted talkgroups still show up in the activity (their calls are assigned in the clear) but can't be heard.

Channel grants

    The control channel is 772.30625 MHz; voice runs on Phase 2 TDMA channels around 772.6–774.7 MHz, all inside the tuner's window. One decoder stays on the control channel and every voice channel in use gets its own decoder, so calls at the same time are all recorded and no grant is missed while a call is decoded. The tab shares the VHF tuner like the Maritime and APRS tabs: it decodes only while the receiver is held here. Talkgroups are numbers (names would need the system's talkgroup list). Live listening plays the calls as dsd-fme decodes them: “Follow activity” stays with the conversation (talkgroup) it's playing, waiting up to 5 s for a reply on any voice channel, and moves to the next one when that goes quiet; picking a talkgroup plays only that one. A voice channel's first call can lose its first second while its decoder starts. Encrypted calls are never followed, so they stay silent. The situation summary is written by a local model (LM Studio, Qwen 3.8 27B) from the transcripts, on this computer; the transcripts are never sent anywhere. To pin its incidents on the map, only the places it names (address, cross streets or business, and city) are looked up with OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, and Overpass for cross streets). Places misheard by the speech recognition can't be placed or land on the wrong spot; a dashed ring marks a pin placed only by its street. Transcripts have errors and the model can misread them, so check the calls behind anything that matters. Recordings stay on this computer and are deleted after 7 days, like the public-safety ones.
