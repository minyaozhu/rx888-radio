700 MHz P25, Live

The region's 700 MHz digital trunked radio system. One control channel assigns each call to a voice channel; dsd-fme decodes the control channel, follows unencrypted calls to their voice channel and records them. Encrypted talkgroups still show up in the activity (their calls are assigned in the clear) but can't be heard.

Channel grants

    The control channel is 772.30625 MHz; voice runs on Phase 2 TDMA channels around 772.6–774.7 MHz, all inside the tuner's window, so following a call is a retune of one radiod channel. The tab shares the VHF tuner like the Maritime and APRS tabs: it decodes only while the receiver is held here. Talkgroups are numbers (names would need the system's talkgroup list). Live listening plays the calls as dsd-fme decodes them: “Follow activity” stays with the call it's playing and moves to the next one when that goes quiet; picking a talkgroup plays only that one. Encrypted calls are never followed, so they stay silent. Recordings stay on this computer and are deleted after 7 days, like the public-safety ones.
