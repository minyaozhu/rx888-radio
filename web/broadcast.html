Broadcast Radio

Bay Area AM stations, shortwave broadcasters from around the world, and Bay Area FM. Scan to see which you can hear right now, click to listen. Shortwave stations are named from the EiBi schedule: who is on that frequency at this hour.

AM / FM station list from Wikipedia's list of Bay Area stations; shortwave from the EiBi schedule (eibispace.de), which lists what is scheduled, not what is audible: the signal column is from your last scan. AM and shortwave use the HF input, FM the VHF tuner; listening or scanning switches the receiver and holds it (the airband / public-safety rotation pauses) until “Resume rotation”. FM plays in stereo when the station sends it (untick “stereo” for mono, quieter on weak stations).
